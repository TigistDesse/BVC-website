Good to know

A few trail
questions.

What should I bring?

Bring water, weather-appropriate layers, sturdy footwear, snacks, and a charged phone. For longer routes, pack the essentials for changing conditions.

Are these trails suitable for beginners?

Meadow Loop is a gentle option. Trail conditions can change, so check current local advice and choose a route that matches your experience.

Can I bring my dog?

Rules vary by trail and park area. Check the current local regulations before bringing a dog, and keep them leashed where required.
